Olive: a working sketch

This is a sketch of how a new Olive website could be organised. It is here so you can click around and see whether the structure makes sense. It is not the finished design, and nothing in it is fixed.

Open the site here: https://davf-git.github.io/olive-2-prototype/

It works best on desktop. It also works on mobile.

What I am trying to do

Olive has built up a lot over the years: hundreds of articles you have chosen and posted, your own writing, your films and installations, exhibitions and events, and the people you have worked with. On the current sites these are spread across different places and are hard to find.

The aim is to give each of these a clear home, so that any visitor can see quickly what Olive is and find their way around.
We want to settle that structure FIRST to avoid time consuming mistakes and confusion later.
Colours, fonts and the look of the site come later, after the structure is fixed.

What you will find

The site has four main sections, plus 'About':

Publish.  Everything Olive has published, newest first. You can search it and narrow it by subject, by kind of piece, by year, or show only Olive's own writing.
Events.   Exhibitions, installations, screenings and talks, by Olive and by the people it works with. Split into coming up and past.
Practice. Artworks: film, installation, music and photography.
About.    What Olive is, the people and organisations involved, projects, a way to propose working together, and contact details.

The names of the sections are working names only. Different words might suits you better but it doesn't matter, they are easy to change.

The line of text over each large picture at the top of a section is there for your own words. You can click on it and type to try something out but what you type is not saved.

What is real and what is a placeholder?
The 61 articles in Publish are real items from olivenetwork.org, with their pictures and opening text. They are a small sample; the full archive has several hundred more.
The pictures at the top of each section are ones you have been using on the current site.
The events, artworks and most names under About are placeholders marked "(sample)". They only show where things would go.
The forms (subscribe, suggest an item, propose a collaboration) do not send anything yet.
Questions we would like your view on
Does the structure make sense? Can you find your way around without help?
Practice: should it show only your own work, or the work of every artist Olive presents?
Section names: do Publish, Events, Practice and About feel right, or would you choose other words?
The lines over the pictures: what would you like to say at the top of each section?
Email updates: would you like people to be able to subscribe to hear about new pieces?
Suggestions from visitors: would you welcome people sending links for you to consider?
Adding new pieces

On the finished site, you would add a new piece of writing by filling in a simple form: a link, title, picture, subject 'tags' and any comment of your own.
To see what that form would look like, add #/add to the end of the site address.



What should happens next?

If the structure feels right to you, we can:

• add the rest of the archive,
• fill in some real events and works,
• set up the form for adding new pieces,
• then turn to the look of the site.

there's also a back end admin suite to lay out and decisions about how people become subscribers. but that's for another day.

Any comments at all are useful, even "I don't understand this bit"
